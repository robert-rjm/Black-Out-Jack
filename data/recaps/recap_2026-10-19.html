Appendix · Definitions

Methodology and definitions

Net sipsEvery sip event after credits (Easy Mode halving, Dealer Lottery, bust-vote, Lucky Sevens, A♣ protection).
Gross sipsAll positive sip events before credits; used for driver mix.
Round playedA round in which the player made at least one decision (decision log).
Clean roundA round played with zero or negative net sips.
Basic-strategy matchShare of hit/stand/double/split decisions equal to the engine's basic-strategy action.
House win rateShare of player hands lost while that player dealt; pushes count as hands.
Driver categoriesOthers' wins: Blackjack bonus, Doubled win, Split win, Suited winning, 5+ card win; Own losing hands: Net hand losses, Lost doubled, Lost suited; Sweeps: Other-player sweep, All-hands sweep; Bust votes: Bust vote wrong, Bust vote handout; Dealer Lottery: Dealer Lottery; Card events: Ace dealt, Four Aces, Devil's Hand, Dealer suited; Dealer duty: Hard Dealer Switch; Milestones: Milestone, Worst average; everything else: Wild Card & other.
